# Privacy Policy — Wealth Habits

*Last updated: September 9, 2026*

## Overview

Wealth Habits ("the app") is a personal finance literacy app. This policy explains what information the app accesses, how it's used, and what stays on your device.

## What the app accesses

- **SMS messages (optional):** If you enable automatic transaction reading, the app reads incoming SMS messages to detect bank transaction alerts. This is processed entirely on your device — message content is never uploaded or transmitted anywhere.
- **Bank statement files (optional):** If you upload a PDF or CSV bank statement, the app extracts transaction data (dates, amounts, descriptions) directly on your device. The original uploaded file is discarded after processing; only the extracted transaction data is kept, stored locally on your device.
- **Google Sign-In (optional):** If you choose to sign in with Google, the app receives your name and profile picture from Google to personalize greetings within the app. This information is stored locally on your device and is not sent to any server operated by the app, because the app has no backend server.

## What the app does NOT do

- The app does not have a backend server and does not transmit your transaction data, SMS content, or bank statement data to any external server.
- The app never requests or stores your bank account number, BVN, card details, or PINs.
- The app does not sell or share your data with third parties.

## Internet access

The app requests internet access solely for:
1. Google Sign-In authentication (handled directly by Google)
2. A one-time download of an on-device text-recognition model (used to read uploaded statement PDFs), provided by Google Play Services

Your financial data itself is never transmitted over the internet by the app.

## Data control

You can delete your locally stored transaction data, revoke SMS permission, or sign out at any time from within the app's Settings screen.
