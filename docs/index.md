# Wealth Habits

Wealth Habits is a personal finance literacy app for Nigerian users. It turns your everyday transactions — logged manually, read from bank SMS alerts, or uploaded from a bank statement — into short, plain-language insights about your spending, without charts or financial jargon.

## What it does

- **Manual entry** — log transactions in a few taps
- **Automatic SMS reading** — recognizes alerts from major Nigerian banks and captures transactions on-device
- **Statement upload** — upload a PDF or CSV bank statement to see a full monthly overview
- **Plain-language insights** — understand what you spent, where, and whether it was a need or a want

## Privacy

Wealth Habits processes your financial data entirely on your device. See our [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md) for details.
